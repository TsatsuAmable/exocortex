# Exocortex Research

Use for research, prior-art review, evidence synthesis, experiments, adversarial evaluation, and claims that may affect project direction.

## Epistemic contract

Separate:
- question or hypothesis;
- observation;
- claim;
- governing criterion;
- inference;
- recommendation or proposal.

Do not turn evidence presence into evidence sufficiency.

Negative, null, contradictory, or inconclusive results are valid outcomes.

## Evidence workflow

1. Define the question and what would change the decision.
2. Search existing GOMS evidence and prior work before acquiring more.
3. Prefer primary sources and inspect provenance.
4. Pre-specify admissible metrics/criteria where practical.
5. Gather only enough evidence to discriminate among live alternatives.
6. Adversarially test assumptions, data quality, selection effects, and counterexamples.
7. Record claims and evidence separately in GOMS.
8. Use **ABSTAIN** when justified decision criteria are absent.

Load grounded-citations, arxiv, or domain-specific research skills when relevant.

## Attention and artefacts

Research exists to improve decisions, not to maximize reading volume.

Prefer reusable artefacts: datasets, evidence bundles, experiment harnesses, bibliographies, structured claims, evaluation reports, and executable protocols.

If new research does not change a decision, capability, uncertainty bound, or reusable knowledge asset, question whether it is worth additional attention.
